RADIOLOGY SUITE — SATURDAY MAINTENANCE WINDOW
Setup notes for the side bay (07:00 – 11:00)

1. Portable unit
   - Warm-up cycle takes approximately four minutes after power-on.
   - Do not take the first exposure until the panel's ready lamp is steady.
   - The unit's battery will not complete a full morning. Keep it on mains power
     between studies and treat any amber battery indication as "return to mains".

2. Plate reader
   - The side bay reader is loaded with the same plate size as the main suite.
   - If the reader's lid status light flashes, reseat the plate before retrying.
   - A plate that fails twice should be set aside and logged; do not keep re-running it.

3. Positioning aids
   - The sandbag set and one trough live in the side bay cupboard for the morning.
   - Return everything to the main suite after the all-clear so the next shift
     finds the cupboards as they expect them.

4. Contact
   - Radiology lead is on site from 07:00. The service engineer is the only person
     in the main suite during the window.
