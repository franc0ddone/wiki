**Owner:** Emergency & Critical Care · **Review cycle:** Every 12 months · **Applies to:** ICU and ER staff who set up oxygen therapy

## Purpose

Set the delivered oxygen and the patient's comfort deliberately, and check the machine rather than trusting the dial's label. Most oxygen-therapy incidents are setup incidents, not patient incidents, and they are caught by a verification step that takes under a minute.

## Before the patient goes in

:::steps
1. **Confirm the order** — the attending veterinarian states the target oxygen percentage and the delivery method, and it is written on the cage card.
2. **Check the oxygen supply** — cylinder pressure or wall line, and confirm the regulator reads what the card says.
3. **Set the flow and the chamber** — cock the door seal, close the latches, and set the humidifier if the order calls for one.
4. **Verify with the meter** — place the oxygen analyser inside the chamber and confirm the reading before the patient is placed, never after.
5. **Settle the patient** — minimal handling, a quiet bay, and the cage front partly covered to reduce stress and keep the patient from overheating.
:::

> [!critical] An oxygen cage without a working analyser is never used. A chamber that reads "about right" by experience is the setup that quietly delivers the wrong concentration, and the patient cannot tell anyone.

## During use

- The analyser reading is charted on every set of rounds alongside the patient's own parameters.
- The chamber is opened as infrequently as possible; every opening is a step back toward room air.
- A patient who is not improving within the time the veterinarian expects is escalated, not simply left in higher flow.

> [!tip] Heat and oxygen interact. A sealed, oxygen-rich chamber also holds heat, so a patient that is already warm has its temperature tracked more often, not less.

## Ventilator setup (ICU only)

Ventilator circuits are set up, tested for leaks, and pre-oxygenated by two staff members together, one of whom is a licence holder. The settings are dictated by the attending clinician and recorded on the ventilator log at the bedside; the log is completed as the settings change, not summarised at the end of the shift.

## Documentation

- The chamber's initial analyser reading, every subsequent reading, and the flow setting are all recorded.
- Any change to the order is attributed to the clinician who made it.
- A patient weaned off oxygen has the weaning sequence charted step by step.
