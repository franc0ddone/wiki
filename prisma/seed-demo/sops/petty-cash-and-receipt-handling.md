**Owner:** Practice Finance · **Review cycle:** Every 12 months · **Applies to:** Staff who hold or disburse the front-desk float

## Purpose

The float exists so that a client can be refunded, a courier paid, or a taxi arranged without a manager present. It is small on purpose, and it balances every day. This procedure is the whole of the handling rule.

## The float

| Item | Value |
| --- | --- |
| Standing float | Fixed at the start of each month and published on the finance board |
| Per-transaction ceiling | Small, and printed on the float envelope |
| Reconciliation | Daily, by the holder and a second signature |

> [!note] The float is reconciled daily even on a day it was not touched. "No movement" is a reconciliation result, and it is written down like any other.

## Daily reconciliation

:::steps
1. **Count the physical float** — notes and coins, against the published standing value.
2. **Match receipts to disbursements** — every amount out has a receipt or a signed voucher.
3. **Write the day's total** — movement, closing balance, and the two signatures on the reconciliation sheet.
4. **Photograph any voucher that is handwritten** — and attach the photograph to the day's entry.
5. **Report a discrepancy the same day** — to the finance manager, before the sheet is filed.
:::

## Receipts and vouchers

- A disbursement without a receipt gets a voucher signed by the person who took the money, with the reason on the face of it.
- Card receipts and printed till rolls are stored with the reconciliation sheet for the month, not loose in the drawer.
- The float is never used to make change for a card payment, and card payments never top the float up.

## Handover

At shift handover, both the outgoing and incoming holder count the float together and both sign. A float is never handed over "as counted" without a count.
