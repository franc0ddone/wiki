**Owner:** Pharmacy & Controlled Substances Committee · **Review cycle:** Every 12 months · **Applies to:** Licensed staff who draw, administer, or waste controlled substances

## Purpose

Controlled substances leave the locked cabinet in a known quantity and must be accounted for in the same quantity when the shift ends. This procedure covers the accounting, not the clinical decision to use the drug.

> [!dosing] This hospital does not publish dose calculations in procedures. Every dose is drawn and administered on the attending veterinarian's individual order, verified against the patient's record at the time of administration, and confirmed by a second licence holder before administration. If an order is ambiguous, ask the prescriber — never infer a dose from a similar case.

## The record

Every movement is a line in the controlled substance register: what was removed, when, by whom, who witnessed, and how much was returned or wasted. The register is the legal record and is never amended by erasing — a correction is a new line that references the line it corrects.

## Withdrawing and administering

:::steps
1. **Read the order aloud** — patient, drug, and the quantity drawn, before the ampoule is opened.
2. **Confirm with your witness** — a second licence holder reads the same order and agrees, then countersigns the register line.
3. **Draw and administer** — the witness stays present until administration is complete.
4. **Record the administered quantity** — in the patient's treatment record and the register line, immediately.
5. **Return the remainder** — the unused volume is returned to the cabinet in a labelled, dated manner, or wasted with the witness still present.
:::

## Wasting

- Wasting is witnessed. An unwitnessed waste is a reportable discrepancy, not a footnote.
- The waste container is never left unsecured and is emptied on a schedule by a licence holder, not by whoever notices it is full.
- A controlled substance is never poured into a general sharps or pharmaceutical waste stream.

> [!critical] Any discrepancy between what the register says left the cabinet and what the shift can account for is reported to the pharmacy lead **the same shift**. A discrepancy found and reported is a clerical problem; a discrepancy found and covered up is a serious matter.

## Shift-end reconciliation

Count the cabinet against the register at every shift change. Both counters sign the sheet, and a mismatch is never left for the incoming shift to resolve.

## Documentation

- Register lines carry the patient's record number, not just a name.
- Witness signatures are full signatures, never initials.
- A wasted ampoule's photograph is attached to the register when the waste is greater than a trace.
