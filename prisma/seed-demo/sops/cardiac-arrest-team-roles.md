**Owner:** Cardiology & Critical Care · **Review cycle:** Every 12 months · **Applies to:** Every clinician and technician who may join a resuscitation

## Purpose

A resuscitation succeeds on role clarity, not on individual heroics. This procedure names the roles, the order they are filled, and the moments at which the team leader deliberately stops the room.

## Activation

Any team member who finds an unresponsive patient calls **"Code Blue"** at full volume and begins compressions immediately. Do not wait for the veterinarian and do not look for the crash cart first — the compressor starts, and somebody else brings the cart.

> [!critical] Announcing a code that turns out not to be one is never a disciplinary matter. Hesitating to announce one always is. If you are wrong, we debrief a mistake; if you are quiet, we lose an animal.

## Roles and responsibilities

Fill roles in the order listed. An unfilled role is a role the team leader calls out loud by name.

| Order | Role | Filled by | Owns |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Compressor | Any staff member present | Continuous compressions, rotating every two minutes |
| 2 | Team leader | Attending veterinarian | The algorithm, the orders, the record |
| 3 | Crash cart | Technician | Drawing and handing off, restocking after |
| 4 | Ventilation | Technician | Airway and bagging once the leader calls for it |
| 5 | Recorder | CSR or technician | The timeline of every order, dose given, and rhythm check |
| 6 | Client liaison | CSR | Moving the client to a quiet room and staying with them |

## Running the room

:::steps
1. **Compressor starts, team leader takes control** — the leader's first words are "compressions are mine to interrupt", and nobody else stops them.
2. **Cart arrives and is opened by the cart technician only** — one person handles drugs so the record matches the ampoules.
3. **The recorder reads back every order** — drug, route, and time, so a missed repeat is caught by the room rather than by the patient.
4. **Compressions pause only on the leader's call** — for rhythm and pulse checks, and for nothing else. The pause is timed and announced.
5. **The leader names the next check out loud** — "rhythm check in one minute" — so the room is not counting in six different heads.
:::

## Rhythm and pause discipline

Compressions are interrupted only for a rhythm or pulse check, at the leader's call, and the leader announces **"pausing"** and **"resuming"** so the recorder can time each gap. Intubation and vascular access happen during a compression cycle, never as their own pause.

## After the code

Within the same shift the team leader convenes a short debrief: what held up, what broke down, and whether the cart is fully restocked. The recorder's sheet goes into the patient record, and a copy goes to the safety officer.
