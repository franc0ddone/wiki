**Owner:** Client Services & Practice Finance · **Review cycle:** Every 12 months · **Applies to:** Client services representatives

## Purpose

Set the financial expectation at the same time as the clinical one, so that a client is never surprised by an estimate and a clinician is never held up by paperwork. Admission is a conversation, and this procedure is the script for it.

## Deposit thresholds

The deposit is calculated from the treatment plan the clinician has already given, not from the presenting complaint. When the clinician is still stabilising a critical patient, admit on the standing emergency deposit and revise it once the plan exists.

| Service | Deposit | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Emergency stabilisation | Standing emergency deposit | Taken before treatment begins; revised after triage |
| Scheduled surgery | Estimated total | Explanation of Benefits issued before the day of surgery |
| Hospitalisation | Per-day estimate | Client updated at each day's start, not only at discharge |
| Outpatient recheck | Payment at visit | No deposit held |

> [!note] A deposit is held against the estimate, not against the final bill. A client paying a deposit has still not agreed to an unlimited total, and every increase to the estimate is authorised by the client before the work is done.

## Registration steps

:::steps
1. **Confirm the client and patient record** — match on the client's phone number, and check for a duplicate record before creating a new one.
2. **Read the estimate aloud** — the range, and specifically what the low end covers and what it does not.
3. **Take the deposit and issue the receipt** — by the method the client chooses, printed or emailed before they leave the desk.
4. **Record the authorised total** — in the estimate field, so the clinician sees the same ceiling the client agreed to.
5. **Hand the client the update commitment** — when they will be called, and by whom.
:::

> [!tip] Ask "what is your budget for today?" before you present the estimate. A plan that fits the client's reality gets authorised in one visit; a plan that does not comes back as a partial payment and a difficult discharge.

## Communication commitments

- A hospitalised patient's client is called at the start of each day with a status and a running total.
- If the estimate will be exceeded, the client is called *before* the additional work is done and the authorisation is recorded against their name.
- A client who declines a recommended plan is offered the alternatives in writing, and their choice is documented.
