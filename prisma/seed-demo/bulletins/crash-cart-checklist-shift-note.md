Overnight found the crash cart's parvo bay bag missing two items the old checklist still claimed were in there. Nothing was used in an arrest, so nothing was missed — the count was simply wrong, and it had probably been wrong for a while.

The restock sheet has been corrected and reprinted. It now lists the bag's contents by compartment rather than as one flat list, so a missing item shows up as a gap in the count you can see rather than a number you have to trust.

## What changed on the sheet

- Items are grouped by the bag's four compartments, in the order you open them.
- Two items that were never in the parvo bag have been removed from it.
- Three items the bag actually carries were added.
- The sheet now has a "sealed, dated, initialled" line per compartment instead of one line for the whole bag.

## If you restock tonight

:::steps
1. **Open the sheet before the bag** — read the compartment you are working on and count against it, not from memory.
2. **Seal and initial each compartment as you finish it** — a completed compartment is signed, not just counted.
3. **Date the bag itself** — the seal date is what tells the next shift whether the count is recent.
4. **Flag anything you could not restock on the sheet** — a missing item is a stock problem, and it needs to be visible rather than silently short.
5. **Hand the sheet to the charge tech at handover** — the completed sheet is the record, not the drawer's contents alone.
:::

> [!note] The old sheets have been pulled from the drawer. If you find one, hand it to the charge technician rather than using it — a checklist that disagrees with the bag is worse than no checklist.

Questions or corrections to the ICU lead.
