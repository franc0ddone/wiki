The exotics ward's ventilation filters are due for their six-monthly change on Tuesday. It is a half-day job in total, but each bay has to be empty while its filter is swapped, so it touches the schedule.

## The plan

- **Bays are done one at a time**, from 08:00, in order of occupancy.
- **Each bay is offline for about an hour**, including a fifteen-minute purge before the patient returns.
- **Patients in transit** are held in the temporary enclosure by the prep area, which is ventilated but warmer than the ward — keep this in mind for the cooler species.

## What you can do to help

If you know a patient will need to be in the ward on Tuesday, tell the exotics technician by Monday evening so the bays can be ordered sensibly. A snake that cannot be moved at short notice should be in the last bay, not the first.

> [!note] The third bay's filter housing has been sticking. If the technician asks for a second pair of hands, it is for that housing, and it takes a few minutes rather than a few seconds.

Thanks — this is maintenance nobody notices when it is done on time, which is exactly the point.
