The upper deck of the staff garage is being resurfaced Monday to Wednesday. It closes at 06:00 on Monday and reopens, weather permitting, on Thursday morning.

## Where to park

- **Staff move to the overflow lot** on the north side, which we have for the three days.
- **Client parking is unaffected** — the short-stay row and the accessible spaces stay as they are.
- **The ambulance bay stays clear.** If the overflow lot fills, do not use the bay; radio the charge technician and use the street until a space frees up.

## Why the timing

The work is happening now because the deck has to cure before the first hard freeze, and doing it in the holiday period would be worse for clients. The contractor has committed to finishing the loudest work before the morning rush, so the first hour of the day should be quiet.

> [!note] If you are on a night shift, your usual exit route through the upper deck will be closed. Use the ground-level exit by the overflow lot — it is signed.

## Anything to flag

If the overflow lot fills before the contractors expect, tell the practice manager rather than moving client vehicles. If the work overruns, this notice is updated rather than replaced.

Thanks for the patience on this one — the deck is genuinely at the end of its life.
