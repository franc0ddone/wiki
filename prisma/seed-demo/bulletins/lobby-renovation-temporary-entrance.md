From Monday morning the west lobby doors are closed for the floor and counter replacement. Client access moves to the south entrance, which is the door the ambulance bay uses after hours.

## What clients will see

- A signed path from the west parking row around to the south entrance, with a covered walkway if it is raining.
- A temporary check-in counter inside the south vestibule, staffed for the full opening hours.
- The comfort room, the lab, and all clinical areas unchanged — only the route in and out moves.

## What staff need to do

- **Meet every taxi and rideshare at the south door.** Clients arriving at the closed west doors have no line of sight to the new entrance.
- **Walk, do not point.** If someone looks lost in the parking row, go to them.
- **Restock the south vestibule the same way the west counter was stocked.** Receipt rolls, pens, and the card terminal all move.

> [!warning] The west doors stay usable as an emergency egress for the duration of the work. They are closed to clients, not locked — do not prop them or stack anything against the crash bars.

## Timeline

The work runs for three weeks and the pinned notice will expire on its own when the lobby reopens. If the contractor's dates move, this notice is updated rather than replaced, so the pinned card stays where people expect it.

Questions to the client services lead.
