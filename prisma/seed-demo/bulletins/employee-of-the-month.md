If you were in the building on the night of the eighteenth, you already know why. Three Level 1s arrived within forty minutes of each other, the treatment floor was full, and the lobby had twelve families in it by nine o'clock.

Priya did not simply keep it moving — she kept it *understandable*. Every client in that room knew roughly where they stood and roughly why. Two of them said so to me the next morning, unprompted, which is the part that earns this notice.

:::spotlight
- **PN** Priya Nandakumar — ER Technician III
:::

## What she actually did

- Ran the door triage herself and escalated the second Level 1 before anyone asked her to.
- Reworked the waiting order twice as patients arrived, and said out loud, each time, what had changed and whether anyone was being pushed back and why.
- Went back to the lobby three times to sit with a client whose dog was on the table, because the clinical team could not and the room had nobody else.
- Handed her own break to a colleague and took a later one in a quiet moment.

Not one of those things is in a procedure. All four are exactly what the procedures assume somebody is already doing.

Congratulations, Priya — and thank you from everyone who was on that shift.
