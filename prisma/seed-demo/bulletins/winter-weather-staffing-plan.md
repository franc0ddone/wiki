The winter weather plan takes effect on Monday and stays in force until spring. It covers the two things that decide whether we can keep the emergency door open: who sleeps near the hospital when a storm is forecast, and who takes the first calls when the pass ices over.

## What changes for you

- **Ride-out shifts are pre-assigned for the season.** If you are on a ride-out list, you will get a text the day before a forecast event, not the morning of.
- **Overnight beds are booked in the on-call room** for anyone on a ride-out list, whether or not you normally work nights.
- **The relief list is opt-in.** Add yourself if you can come in on short notice, and take yourself off it when your circumstances change — both are respected equally.

> [!note] Being on the relief list is not a promise to answer every call. It only means the coordinator is allowed to ask.

## If you cannot get in

Call the charge technician, not the front desk. The charge tech maintains the running sheet of who is in the building and who is on their way, and the front desk will only route you to them anyway.

If the storm closes the main roads, the hospital has arranged priority access through the transit corridor. Details are posted at the staff entrance and repeated in the storm text.

## Questions

Talk to your service lead, or bring it to the Friday huddle. The plan is reviewed after every storm that triggers it, and any part of it that did not work is worth saying out loud.
