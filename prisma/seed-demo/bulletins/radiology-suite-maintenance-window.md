The main radiology suite is booked for its annual generator service on Saturday from 07:00 to 11:00. The equipment is fine — this is scheduled maintenance, and the suite will likely be back before 11:00.

## During the window

- **The main suite is closed.** Do not enter to collect a plate or to use the viewing station.
- **Emergency films move to the side bay.** The portable unit and its plate reader are set up there from 06:45.
- **The viewing station outside the suite stays live**, so images can still be reviewed in the usual place.
- **Dental and non-urgent studies are deferred** to Saturday afternoon or Monday, and these should already have been rebooked.

> [!warning] No one except the service engineer is in the suite during the window. The interlock is deliberately live, and the door will not open for a staff badge — that is the point.

## What you need to do

Pull any studies you expect to need on Saturday morning *before* Friday night, while the archive is at full speed. If you are working the side bay, the attached note has the setup and the portable unit's warm-up timings.

## After the window

The engineer signs the suite back over to the radiology lead, who clears it for use. Do not take the first film until the lead has posted the all-clear on the day's channel.
