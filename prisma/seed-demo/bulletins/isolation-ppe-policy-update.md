The weekly isolation audit has been telling us the same thing for a month: the ward's rules are right, but two of them read ambiguously enough that people guess. Both have been rewritten in the procedure, and both are now in force as written.

## What changed

- **Gowning order is now explicit.** Gown first, then gloves with the cuffs over the gown sleeve. The procedure previously said "gown and gloves" and left the order to the reader.
- **Hand hygiene is now twice at the doorway.** Once gloved, once bare-handed. The old wording said "at the doorway", and the bare-handed step was regularly skipped on the way out.

## Why it matters

Neither change is a new rule — both describe what the ward was already supposed to do. The rewrite removes the gap between what the audit measures and what the procedure says, which is the gap people fall into when they are busy.

> [!critical] The doffing order has not changed and is not negotiable: gloves, then gown, then eye protection, then shoe covers, with hand hygiene in between. Doffing in the wrong order is how a clean hand picks up what the gown just shed.

## What to do now

Read the updated procedure before your next ward entry. If anything in it is still ambiguous after reading it twice, tell me — the point of this revision is that the next person does not have to guess.
