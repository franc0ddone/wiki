On November 5 we are running a client education evening for families whose pets are in or have finished oncology treatment. It is deliberately informal: a short talk, then as much question time as the room wants.

## The evening

- **When:** November 5, 18:30 for 19:00, finishing by 20:30.
- **Where:** the education room off the south corridor.
- **Who:** clients, plus any staff member who wants to hear the questions clients actually ask when the clinician has left the room.

## Why we are doing it

The questions that worry clients most are rarely the ones they ask us in the consult room. They ask their friends, or a forum, or nobody. An hour in a comfortable room, with other people who are in the same position, does more for a client's confidence than a leaflet ever has.

Light refreshments, no charge, and clients are welcome to bring someone with them.

> [!tip] If you are on the desk when a client mentions the evening, offer to put their name down even if they seem unsure. The room is worst when it is half empty, and a tentative yes converts far better than a follow-up call.

## For staff

Anyone who attends and would rather not be "on" is welcome to sit at the back and just listen — being there is the contribution. If you can help set up or greet, tell the client services lead.
