We have had three confirmed feline panleukopenia cases in ten days, all presenting through the front door with unverified vaccination history. Two of the three walked into the lobby before triage flagged them. Until this notice expires, the following changes apply to every feline presenting with acute GI signs or a fever of unknown origin.

## What changes at intake

- **Any cat with acute vomiting, diarrhoea, or a fever of unknown origin is triaged at the door.** Do not bring it into the lobby to register.
- **Intake moves to the side-door bay** used for the parvo protocol, with the same one-way route.
- **SNAP testing happens in the isolation prep area only**, never on the central treatment table.
- **Cage cards are flagged on arrival**, before the patient is settled, not after the first test.

> [!critical] A cat is not cleared from isolation on a single negative test. Clearance follows the isolation protocol's criteria, and the attending clinician documents the decision. If you are unsure, treat the patient as positive until the clinician says otherwise.

## What we need from everyone

Read this notice and acknowledge it, so we know the message reached the shifts that need it. If you work with a team member who has not seen it, walk them through the intake change at the door.

The full barrier and doffing rules are unchanged and are in the linked procedure. This notice is about *who* goes into isolation, not *how* the ward works.
