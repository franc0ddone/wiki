The chemotherapy handling reference has been reprinted and laminated at the prep bench, replacing the older sheet that had drifted out of date on three points. It is one page, and it is meant to be read standing up.

## What it covers

- **Spill response**, in the order the steps have to happen, including what to do with a spill that reaches the floor drain.
- **Waste segregation**, and specifically what does *not* go into the cytotoxic bin.
- **The extravasation plan**, as a single page, so that nobody is hunting for the procedure mid-incident.

> [!critical] The reference is a summary, not a substitute for the procedure. During an actual extravasation, follow the procedure and call the oncologist — do not work from the laminated sheet, which omits the escalation steps by design.

## A note on the old sheet

The old version is in the recycling, not in a drawer. If you find one anywhere on the service, bin it — two references that disagree is the situation this reprint exists to end.
