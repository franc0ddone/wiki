The monthly audit of the controlled substance log found four entries this period signed by one licence holder. None of the four involved a missing quantity, and all four were corrected the same shift — but a single-signed line is a reportable discrepancy regardless of how it turns out.

## The rule, plainly

- **Every withdrawal and every waste is witnessed by a second licence holder.** Not "usually" — every one.
- **The witness is present for the action**, not signing afterwards from the record. A signature added later is not a witness.
- **A waste is never deferred** to the end of the shift and reconstructed. If the witness is with another patient, wait for them.

> [!warning] If you genuinely cannot find a second licence holder, the correct answer is to wait. Administering on an unwitnessed withdrawal is the reportable event; a delay is not. Escalate to the charge clinician if a patient cannot wait.

## Why the reminder

This is the kind of rule that frays quietly. It frays on a busy night, when everyone is competent, and the person drawing up is also the most experienced clinician in the room. The audit exists precisely because competence makes the shortcut feel safe.

Please reread the procedure before your next involvement with the cabinet. It is short, and the reconciliation section in particular is worth five minutes.
